Adds `UploadZone` to the widget catalog (`@cosmicdrift/kumiko-renderer-web`) — a drop-zone/multi-file-picker widget with per-file upload status, for apps that need a proper upload surface without reaching for raw HTML tags (fw#1831). `ButtonProps` also gains optional `className`/`ref` so a `Button` can be composed into custom drop targets.
