The French and Italian locale packages are removed; framework copy ships in English, German and Spanish.

<!-- kumiko-changes
feature: framework
type: breaking
title: locale-fr and locale-it packages removed
detail: `@cosmicdrift/kumiko-locale-fr` and `@cosmicdrift/kumiko-locale-it` are no longer published; no app mounted them. Framework screens and mails ship in English, German (`locale-de`) and Spanish (`locale-es`), and a French or Italian request falls back to English. Both packages can be regenerated by AI translation of the English catalog once an app needs them.
migration: Remove `localeFr()`, `localeFrClient()`, `localeIt()` and `localeItClient()` and the two package.json dependencies. An app that needs French or Italian generates its own locale package from the English catalog.
affects: @cosmicdrift/kumiko-locale-fr, @cosmicdrift/kumiko-locale-it, localeFr, localeFrClient, localeFrBundle, localeIt, localeItClient, localeItBundle
-->

<!-- kumiko-changes
feature: agent-tools
type: breaking
title: kumiko api-docs generates en, de and es
detail: `apiDocsLocales` is `["en", "de", "es"]` and `ApiDocsLocale` no longer contains `"fr"` and `"it"`. `apiDocs.locales` or `--locales` with fr or it fails with the unsupported-locale message instead of writing French or Italian pages.
migration: Drop "fr" and "it" from `apiDocs.locales` in kumiko.config.ts.
affects: apiDocsLocales, ApiDocsLocale, isApiDocsLocale, apiDocs.locales
-->

<!-- kumiko-changes
feature: tenant

affects: tenant:config:locale
-->
