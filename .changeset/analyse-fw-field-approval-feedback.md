The field approval says things once. The edit form shows one warning banner that names the gated fields and offers "Submit for approval"; the field itself only carries a "Needs approval" marker instead of an error text, and submitting confirms with a success notice. The approval list shows who requested a change to approvers who are no admins, and its phone card puts the current and the new value on their own labelled lines. An open request stays visible at its field after a reload and names the requested value, and an empty approval list says that nothing awaits approval. The approval screen and its nav entry now also reach the roles that may file a request, so more people see the "Approvals" entry.
