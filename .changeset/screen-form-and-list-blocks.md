New screen options for forms, lists and record details: `aiAssist` (stackable chips and a free instruction on a text field that call a configurable query) and `valueChips` (quick values on a number or money field) on form fields, `fieldDescriptions` and `formErrorOnTop` on actionForm, `pillField`/`pillToneField` and `format: "phone"` on list columns, `footerActions` on projectionDetail, `photoGrid` on relatedList, and `icon` on the record summary. The flaky Replace step of write-only fields is fixed. projectionDetail tabs can stack several sections (fields, relatedList, the new `stepList`) under one tab and hide a tab with `visibleWhen`; actionForm gets `infoBand`, `fieldBadges` and chip multi-selects (`display: "chips"`); an empty list no longer shows an entry-count footer; the drawer is a bottom sheet on phones.
