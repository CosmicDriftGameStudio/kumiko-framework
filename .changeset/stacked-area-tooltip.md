Stacked-area charts get a tooltip, gradient bands and staggered marker pins

Hovering the plot (mouse) or tapping it (touch tap, not a swipe; a second tap, a tap outside or Escape closes) shows a card with the bucket date, every series and every forecast line with its compact formatted value. The bands fade from top to bottom and have a crisp top edge. Marker pins that sit close together are staggered into up to three rows instead of overlapping. Stacked-bar and timeseries charts use the compact currency y axis as well ("1,5 Mio. €").
