More declarative screen options for boards. Dashboards take `layout: "main-side"` with `column: "side"` or `column: "top"` (full width above both columns) on panels, a text panel takes `icon`, a list column with `display: "button"` takes `variant: "primary"`, and a stat-group child with `hideOnNarrow` is now left out on phones (a group may not hide all of its children). The steps panel of a projectionDetail takes `moreActionsLabel` and `visibleWhen`, projectionDetail takes `initialTab`, `footerHint` and renders `slots.afterForm` (which receives `activeTabId`); `footerActions` stay in the phone footer instead of folding into the more-menu. Forms take `layout.allRequiredHint: false`, and actionForm `fieldLabels` and `fieldDescriptions` accept `{ key, params }` filled from form values. The AI prompt input submits with Enter (the Apply button is gone), `valueChips` show formatted values and mark the current one, and a client row action takes `successMessage: false`.
